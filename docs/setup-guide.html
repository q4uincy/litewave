litewave Spotify setup

Takes about 5 minutes, and you only do it once. You need a Spotify Premium account: since February 2026 Spotify only lets Premium accounts create developer apps, and playing music needs Premium too. Without Premium you can still play your own music files in litewave.

Why? Spotify limits how many requests one app can make. By making your own app, litewave gets its own allowance, so it does not get stuck with "too many requests".

1 Open the Spotify developer dashboard

Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account. Accept the terms if it asks.

2 Create an app

Press Create app and fill in:

App namelitewave (anything works)
App descriptionMusic player (anything works)
Redirect URIhttp://127.0.0.1:8898/login then press Add. It must match exactly.
APIs usedTick Web API

Tick the terms box and press Save.

3 Copy the Client ID

Open your new app. The Basic Information tab shows your keys. Copy the Client ID (32 letters and numbers).

Do not copy or share the Client secret. litewave does not need it.

4 Add yourself as a user

Still in the app's settings, open User management. Add your own name and the email of your Spotify account, then press Add user. (New apps are in "development mode" and only work for people on this list.)

5 Sign in inside litewave

Open litewave, click Connect Spotify in the sidebar (it is called Spotify account once you are connected), paste the Client ID, and press Sign in with Spotify. Your browser opens: approve litewave. You approve twice, once for browsing and once for playback.

Please wait a moment after pressing Sign in. A new browser tab opens by itself to connect Spotify, and it can take a few seconds. Do not press the button again. When the tab says litewave is connected, you can close it and go back to the app.

When the card says Connected, you are done. Your phone will also show a device called litewave.

Problems?

Browser says INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URIThe Redirect URI in the dashboard must be exactly http://127.0.0.1:8898/login. Check that you pressed Add and then Save.
"Port 8898 is busy"Close the old sign-in browser tab, then try again.
Spotify API error 429Make sure your own Client ID is pasted. Wait a minute, then refresh.
Nothing happens after pressing Sign inWait a few seconds, the browser tab opens by itself. If it still does not, close any old sign-in tab and press the button once more.
Sign-in worked but nothing playsPlayback needs Spotify Premium.
Some playlists (like Blend or Daily Mix) are missingSpotify does not let apps in development mode read playlists Spotify makes. Playlists you own or share with others work.
litewave is an unofficial player and is not made by or connected to Spotify. Spotify can change or block it at any time.
